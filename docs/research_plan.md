# Customer Research Plan

## Objective
Validate which checkout uncertainties most affect shopper confidence and completion.

## Research methods
1. 5–8 semi-structured interviews across first-time and repeat fashion shoppers.
2. Short survey to rank checkout friction.
3. Moderated usability test of a clickable prototype.
4. Review of public feedback, coded by theme and date.

## Interview prompts
- Tell me about your most recent online fashion purchase.
- At what point, if any, did you hesitate or leave checkout?
- When did you first understand the total payable amount?
- How do you decide whether delivery timing is acceptable?
- Describe the last time a coupon did not work as expected.
- What information would have made the decision easier?

## Analysis
Code responses by theme, severity, frequency and evidence confidence. Separate observed behaviour from participant opinion. Do not generalise from a small qualitative sample.

## Ethics
Obtain consent, avoid collecting payment credentials or unnecessary personal data, and anonymise notes.
